Fragateiro

Before bridges and engines, more than 3600 sailing boats worked on the Tagus River. Fewer than 150 remain. These small games let you sail four of them: the Falua, a fast passenger and cargo boat with two lateen sails; the Muleta of Seixal, which fished by drifting sideways under a crowd of sails; the Fragata, the big cargo barge of the river; and the small Catraio.

The boats

Falua

A fast boat that carried passengers across the Tagus and light cargo to the Lisbon markets. It shares its two triangular lateen sails, and its name (from the Arabic falūka, "small boat"), with the Egyptian felucca. A crew of two or three. No original Falua survives, and nobody alive remembers seeing one.

14–18 m · about 15 t · two lateen sails · crew of 2–3

Muleta

The legendary fishing boat of Seixal, known since the 15th century. It fished by drifting sideways before the wind, dragging its net from long booms at the bow and stern, with up to twelve sails set to balance the drift. The last Muleta disappeared at the end of the 19th century and the technique has been almost entirely lost. In the game, try its lateral fishing mode.

10–12 m · about 7 t · up to 12 sails · crew of about 14

Fragata

The big sailing barge of the Tagus, which carried cargo between Lisbon and the towns along the river. A raked mast carries a large gaff mainsail and a jib. A few fragatas have been restored and still sail on the river.

about 18–25 m · about 30 t part-loaded · gaff mainsail and jib

Catraio

A small Tagus boat for passengers and goods. Its mainsail is held up by a diagonal spar, the sprit (espicha), with a jib on the bowsprit and a small mizzen at the stern.

about 10 m · about 5 t · spritsail, jib and mizzen

Canoa under development

A narrow Tagus boat with one big triangular lateen sail on a long yard, its tack on a bowsprit, and a small mizzen at the stern. Restored canoas still sail on the river. Its 3D model, built from a photo and its dimensions, is still being improved: it is not in the game yet.

8.8 m · 2.2 m beam · about 5 t · lateen sail and mizzen

Fragateiro

Before bridges and engines, more than 3600 sailing boats worked on the Tagus River. Fewer than 150 remain. These small games let you sail two of them — the Falua, a fast passenger and cargo boat with two lateen sails, and the Muleta of Seixal, which fished by drifting sideways under a crowd of sails.

Sailing the Tagus

Free sailing with the Falua and the Muleta. Trim the sails, steer, and try the Muleta's lateral fishing.
